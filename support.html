Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

📷 How do I position my camera for accurate push-up counting?

Place your phone on the floor or a sturdy stand about 1.5 to 2.5 meters away, tilted slightly upward so your entire head, arms, and torso are clearly visible in the camera frame. Make sure you are in a well-lit room.

⚡ How do Battle Tokens work?

Free players receive 5 free battle tokens every day. Workout Wars PRO members receive unlimited battle tokens with no daily caps.

💳 How do I restore or manage my PRO Subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via your device's Apple ID (iOS Settings → Subscriptions) or Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & Subscriptions). You can also tap "Restore Purchases" on the Workout Wars paywall screen.

🗑️ How can I delete my account and data?

Open the app, navigate to Settings → Delete Account to immediately wipe your account, profile, and match stats. Alternatively, email ahmadahsanr@gmail.com with your player ID and we will delete your records.
