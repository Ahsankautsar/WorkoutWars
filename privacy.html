Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: AUGUST 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Workout Wars ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. Workout Wars is developed and operated by Amika Studio. By installing or using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: A unique player ID generated anonymously. If you optionally choose to link your account via Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address to maintain your progress across devices.
  • Profile & Game Data: Your warrior display name, optional profile photo selected from your device, player challenge code, match history, repetitions, win/loss stats, and rank tier.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device token used via Firebase Cloud Messaging solely to alert you of incoming match invites or rematch challenges when the app is closed.
  • Diagnostics & Analytics: Anonymous crash logs and performance telemetry collected through Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix bugs.

3. Camera & Computer Vision Privacy

Rep counting is performed 100% on your device: Your camera frames are processed locally by on-device computer vision models to detect push-up motion angles. Live video feeds are never recorded or uploaded to our servers.

During active live multiplayer matches, low-resolution camera snapshots are transmitted peer-to-peer to your match rival(s) in real time to verify physical presence. This transmission stops immediately when the match concludes.

4. How We Use Information

  • To power real-time PvP matchmaking, lobbies, and multiplayer battles.
  • To maintain your player stats, streaks, and global ranked leaderboard positions.
  • To process optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
  • To diagnose technical errors and improve app performance.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third parties. We utilize industry-standard cloud infrastructure provided by Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Analytics) and official Apple / Google payment subsystems, which operate under strict security and data protection standards.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can manage or delete your data at any time directly within the app under Settings → Delete Account, which removes your profile and match data from our database. You can also request complete data deletion by emailing us at ahmadahsanr@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Workout Wars is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for removal.
